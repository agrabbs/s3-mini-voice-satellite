⚠️ Before You Begin

  • Home Assistant 2023.10 or later installed
  • Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium-based browser (on desktop, not mobile)
  • ESP32-S3 Mini board with components soldered according to wiring guide
  • USB-C cable for data transfer (not just charging)
  • Your 2.4GHz WiFi network name and password ready
Important: Make sure all hardware is connected according to the wiring guide before flashing the firmware. Incorrect wiring may damage components.

Install Firmware

Click the button below to begin installation. Your browser will prompt you to select the serial port.

Installation typically takes 2-5 minutes

Installation Steps

1

Connect Device

Plug your ESP32-S3 Mini into your computer using a USB-C cable. Click "CONNECT & INSTALL" above.

2

Select Serial Port

When the popup appears, select the port that shows your ESP32-S3 device and click "Connect".

3

Install Firmware

Click "Install" and wait for the firmware to be written to your device. Don't disconnect during this process!

4

Configure WiFi

Enter your WiFi network credentials when prompted. The device will connect to your network.

5

Add to Home Assistant

The device will be automatically discovered by Home Assistant. Go to Settings → Devices & Services to complete setup.

6

Configure Voice Assistant

Follow the Home Assistant wizard to select your wake word, voice pipeline, and test the device.

First Time Using This Device? Make sure you've followed the complete wiring guide and have all required components connected properly. Check the README for the complete setup process.

Troubleshooting

No Serial Port Appears

Your computer may be missing a USB driver. Try installing the CH342/CH343 or CP2102 driver for your operating system.

Installation Fails

Make sure you're using a data-capable USB cable (not charge-only). Try a different cable or USB port.

Device Not Discovered by Home Assistant

Check that both your computer and Home Assistant are on the same network. Wait a few minutes for automatic discovery, or manually add the ESPHome integration.
